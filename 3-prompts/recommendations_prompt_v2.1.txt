Now turn the Content GEO Score above into recommendations.

INPUTS
- Primary business objective (required; ask once if missing): e.g. "more qualified leads for liquid cooling", "be cited for 'what is SF6-free switchgear'".
- Optional: competitor pages, target role, the CTA you want readers to take.

RULES
1. Never invent facts: no made-up numbers, quotes, customers, certifications or dates. Where a fact is needed, write a placeholder: [VERIFY: what to check] or [SME: what to ask].
2. Restructure before rewriting. Keep the page's own wording for its topic and key terms; move, split, label and tighten first.
3. Fix what failed, not what passed. Each recommendation must name the failed check it fixes.
4. No manipulation: no fake or refreshed dates without a real content change, no hidden or AI-directed text, no keyword stuffing, no self-ranking "best of" lists.
5. Write naturally and vary structure. Do not paste the same FAQ, statistic or template block into every section; formulaic "GEO" patterns are detectable and add no value.
6. Prioritise: score = recoverable points x fit with the business objective / effort (S = under 1 hour, M = half a day, L = needs an SME, a customer approval or a template change). Fix gate-triggering problems first.

FORMAT (top 5-7, highest priority first). For each:
- Title: what to do, in one line.
- Fixes: criterion and check (e.g. U5 c1), level now -> target, estimated points gained.
- Effort: S / M / L. Owner: author [A], template/CMS team [T], or subject expert [SME].
- Where: the section or element.
- BEFORE: the current text (quote it).
- AFTER: the rewritten text, with [VERIFY]/[SME] placeholders where facts are needed.
- How we'll know: the check that will pass after the change.

Then add a one-line "Beyond this page" note only if relevant (earned coverage, partner content, entity consistency); advise, do not score.
End with: "Do you want more recommendations, or a full rewrite of one section?"
