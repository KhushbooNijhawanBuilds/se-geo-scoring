# SE House GEO Frameworks (from GEO Playbook v2026-Jun-01 and GEO Functional Toolkit v1.0, Aug 2026)

Use this vocabulary in all outputs so they line up with the internal playbooks. The full synthesis is in `GEO Elec Project/research/00-internal-source-synthesis.md`.

- **GEO definition:** making content work for the AI reasoning layer that assembles answers. GEO runs in parallel to SEO and does not replace it. *"When AI answers a customer's question, does it mention or recommend us?"* AI-ready = **accessible, structured, answer-shaped**.
- **KPIs:** Visibility Score (foundation) · **Share of Voice** and **Citation Share** (primary) · Sentiment (supporting). Engines tracked: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot. Tool: Profound.
- **Execution loop:** Setup (topics × prompts × personas × competitors → baseline) → Analyze (gaps) → Optimize & Generate → repeat. Don't skip phases.
- **Three-Filter topic test:** commercially significant · queried · competitively contested.
- **Prompt = Topic × Persona × Stage × Intent.** 15–20 prompts per topic.
- **THE THREE GATES:**
  - Gate 1 *Fetchable*: tech, out of scope.
  - Gate 2 *Chosen*: URL, title, headers and meta signal the answer. Only the **wording** is in scope.
  - Gate 3 *Extractable*: answer in the first 100 words, H2/H3 = fan-out sub-queries, factual density, tables/lists. **Core scope.**
- **Five-Dimension Content Audit:** Relevance · Factual Density · Semantic Structure · Recency · Authority. These map to U1/U6 · U4 · U3 · U9 · U5.
- **Five moves to optimize an existing page:**
  1. Match the prompt in the headers.
  2. Write a 40–60-word answer paragraph.
  3. Inject factual density.
  4. Add structural clarity (lists, steps, tables).
  5. Remove the fluff.

  *Optimization is often subtraction.*
- **Answer-shaped paragraph:** subject + primary function → specific citable differentiator → target application/audience. Example: "The RM AirSeT is an SF6-free medium voltage switchgear that uses pure air technology, designed for commercial buildings and utilities."
- **Banner/hero 4-question test:** what it is · who it's for · when it applies · what outcome it delivers. These are U2.
- **GEO Gate before publishing:**
  - Clear page definition at the top.
  - Audience and scenario stated.
  - Outcomes listed.
  - Content is fact-based and extractable.
  - No critical info behind forms.
- **"Would an AI confidently quote this sentence?"** and **"Gate the deep dive, not the knowledge."**
- **CTA rule:** descriptive and intent-based ("Download the energy management guide"), never "Learn more" / "Get started".
- **Cadence:**
  - FAQ: review every 90–120 days, and immediately after product or policy changes.
  - Blogs: refresh at least yearly.
  - Documents: audit twice a year, and after any product or firmware revision.
  - General content: every 6–12 months.
- **Citation economy:** about 80% of AI citations are third-party. Of those, about 50% come from the channel ecosystem, about 30% from earned/social/industry media, and about 20% from other sources. **Consensus Principle:** independent corroboration of the same claim.
- **Partner GEO Content Kit:**
  - A 50–70-word answer-shaped description.
  - An HTML spec table of the top 8–10 specs.
  - 3–5 FAQs.
  - Application context plus a canonical link.
  - Authority templates.
- **Diagnostics:**
  - Mention vs citation gaps: *Authority gap* (high mentions, low citations), *Awareness gap* (low on both), *Positioning gap* (cited but framed as secondary).
  - Visibility drop protocol: verify → check the gates → analyze who replaced us → fix (technical, content or authority).
  - Sentiment root causes: perception problem · messaging gap · reality gap · hallucination.
- **Net-new vs optimize:** check format match, whether density is sufficient, and whether the gap is answered anywhere. If not, build a **Scalpel Page**. The **New Content Brief** covers target query · fan-out cluster · audience · format · required facts · prohibited language · success criterion.
- **Technical docs:** HTML-first, PDF-also. That means a gateway page, the PDF embedded, schema, and the top 5–10 specs in HTML. Use stable URLs, descriptive filenames and PDF metadata.
- **Existing house scores:**
  - *GEO Strategic Score (prompt prioritization):* Competitive Density, Citation Share Potential, Business Impact and Technical Feasibility/E-E-A-T, each 0–25.
  - *Web Page Assessment:* AI Interpretability 30%, Semantic Richness 25%, Conversational Relevance 20%, Structural Optimization 15%, Generative Engagement 10%, scored 0–5 → /5×100.
  - Bands: <40 Poor · 40–69 Average · 70–89 Good · 90+ Excellent.
  - The Content GEO Score keeps the bands and the 0–5 scale. It replaces the technical and lagging components with content-only criteria.
- **UX principles:**
  - Design for the answer, with a dual audience: humans and AI.
  - Every page is a self-sufficient entry point built from modular, citable blocks.
  - Query-matched H1–H3, at most 3 levels deep.
  - Paragraphs of 3–4 sentences or fewer; tables for anything with more than 2 dimensions.
  - One term per concept.
  - Templates: GEO Power Page · GEO Optimized Template · GEO Power Couple.
  - PDP, range and category data often comes from PIM/OfferDAM, so route those fixes to the PIM champion.
- **Video:**
  - Publish on YouTube **and** embed on a crawlable SE page.
  - Answer within 30–90 s, with human-reviewed .SRT captions.
  - Question-style chapters starting at 00:00, with at least 3.
  - A description of 300+ words with a link to the SE page.
- **Images:** alt text of 80–140 characters that describes the image's purpose; an explanatory paragraph and caption; descriptive filenames; never put critical info only in the image.
- **Social and community:** executive and employee voice outweighs brand voice. Participate on Reddit as named, disclosed contributors who answer questions rather than advertise. Expect 6+ months to build authority.
- **Linking:** link by entity to one canonical page, with descriptive anchors. Hubs open with a 40–60-word definition. No orphan pages. External **mentions** matter more than links.
