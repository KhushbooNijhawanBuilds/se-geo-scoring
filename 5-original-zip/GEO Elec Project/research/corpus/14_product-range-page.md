# Discover the Galaxy V series

Galaxy V series of 3-phase UPSs—with its first-class power protection, business continuity, scalability, and operational efficiency—solves today’s energy challenges while setting the standard for quality and innovation with fully integrated solutions.

Manage your AI load while pursuing your sustainability goals with Galaxy VXL. With AI load-tolerant design, industry-leading compact footprint, efficiency up to 99% in eConversion mode, and fast scalability with Live Swap, Galaxy VXL is the ultra high-density backbone of the digitized world, ideal for large and hyperscale data centers, and commercial and critical infrastructure.

Galaxy VX is a highly efficient, modular 3-phase UPS with high performance and flexible operating modes. Its scalability accommodates the changing needs of your rapidly expanding business.
