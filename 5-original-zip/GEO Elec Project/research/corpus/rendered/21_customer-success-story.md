## Sustainability in focus across the board

Massmart is a major retailer owned by Walmart and headquartered in South Africa. Over the last decade, we have worked with them to integrate EcoStruxure Building Operation into more than 250 of their stores, achieving significant energy savings and unprecedented visibility across their operations.

[hidden] ## Visibility and energy savings across operations

[hidden] With a diverse range of stores spanning across Sub-Saharan Africa, Massmart required a sustainability solution that would give them comprehensive oversight and control over all building management, power usage, and other operational factors, while also reducing costs and enhancing efficiency – no matter the store format.

[hidden] ![man working on machinery]

[hidden] ## A one-size-fits-all solution

[hidden] Massmart partnered with Schneider Electric and Green Wave, a master-level EcoXpert partner, to implement EcoStruxure Building Operation, our next generation building management system (BMS), across their store network, creating a unified system for building and power management.

[hidden] ![two men pointing at screen]

[hidden] ## Real results in real-time

[hidden] Massmart's deployment of EcoStruxure Building Operation has yielded significant energy savings, achieving annualized energy cost reductions of R15 million (ZAR). The comprehensive BMS has empowered Massmart with unparalleled visibility and real-time actionable insights, enabling them to identify inefficiencies, uncover billing discrepancies, and streamline its response to system alerts, all while delivering a strong return on investment.
