## Where data meets drive

## The challenge

Help provide reliable and resilient power across McLaren Racing’s footprint, from its headquarters in Woking, UK, to the new McLaren Racing Center in Indianapolis, and to demanding trackside environments around the world.

![A dusk photograph of a modern, curved glass building beside a reflective water feature.]

## The solution

- Our medium- and low-voltage switchgear enhance power reliability and efficiency, while energy metering brings visibility into the quality and cost of that power.

- Open, interoperable EcoStruxure™ software helps McLaren Racing optimize energy usage while reducing costs.

- These solutions scale across McLaren Racing’s entire operations, on and off the track.

![A technician wearing a high-visibility safety vest operating an electrical switchgear.]

## The results

By lowering costs and optimizing its infrastructure for greater digitalization, efficiency, and automation, McLaren Racing can channel more resources into on-track performance.
