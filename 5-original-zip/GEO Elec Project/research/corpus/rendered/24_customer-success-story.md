## A passion for coffee and innovation

For nearly a century and across four generations, Sicilian coffee company Zicaffè has been driven by an entrepreneurial spirit and a focus on innovation. Today, it continues on this path by adopting the latest in open automation and next-gen digital solutions from Schneider Electric, expertly implemented by Alliance system integrator partner BitControl.

## Boost manufacturing agility and efficiency

Many of the complex automation challenges faced by the consumer packaged goods industry can be solved with digitalization. However, the decades-old technology models currently in use may need to be updated to support this transformation. EcoStruxure™ Automation Expert, the open, software-defined automation solution, can make the difference.
