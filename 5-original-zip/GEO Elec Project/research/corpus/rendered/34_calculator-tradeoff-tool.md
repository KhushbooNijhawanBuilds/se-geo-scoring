# Single-phase UPS efficiency comparison calculator

Use this TradeOff Tool to analyze the efficiency of one or two UPSs and gain insights on how these efficiencies impact electricity cost and carbon footprint. The calculator enables you to make informed decisions about which UPS best meets your energy-saving goals and operational needs for critical single-phase UPS applications like Gen AI inferencing at the edge.

[hidden] The summary table is computed at the load set by the vertical line in the graph above. It provides the UPS efficiency, UPS losses, energy, carbon footprint, and electricity cost for UPS A, UPS B (if chosen), and the difference between the two, if a comparison was done. Note - These results do not include the energy cost or carbon footprint of the load itself, only the losses associated with the UPS system.

[hidden] Performance comparison

[hidden] To begin a comparison, select the two UPS models to be compared. The pull down lists allow you to filter to your choices of UPSs (Family, Configuration, Ratings, Model, and Conditions), or you can select 'User defined' and define a UPS based on data that you have available. You can also analyze a single UPS by setting UPS B to 'Analyze Only UPS A'.

[hidden] Note, models with configuraitons that don't specify a country or region are available in multiple regions.

[hidden] UPS A and UPS B attributes

[hidden] The purpose of this tool is to compare the efficiences of two UPS systems and to show the impact these efficiencies have on electricity cost and carbon footprint. UPSs may be selected from a pull down list, or users can define their own UPS (Schneider Electric or other vendor). Prepopulated data was obtained by curve fitting to measured efficiency data.

[hidden] The tool's results include graphs that illustrate the UPS systems' efficiency, electricity cost, losses, and carbon emissions as a function of load, and the values and differences of these variables at a load selected by the user (with the vertical line on the graphs).
