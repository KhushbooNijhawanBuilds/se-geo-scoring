## What is EcoCare?

EcoCare is your answer to the high cost of planned and unplanned downtime, resource gaps, and inefficient maintenance, enabling the shift from time-based to proactive, condition-based maintenance. It’s a digital-first service plan that combines 24/7 remote monitoring with AI-powered condition-based maintenance — backed by dedicated experts — to help maximize uptime, extend asset lifespan, and streamline operational efficiency. With EcoCare, you'll unlock exclusive benefits including:

- Faster, personalized, advanced technical support, and success plan

- Break-fix emergency intervention

- Discount rates on training, spare parts, and on-site service
