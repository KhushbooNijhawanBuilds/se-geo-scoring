## What is DCIM?

Data center infrastructure management (DCIM) software is used to monitor, measure, and manage data centers, covering both IT equipment and supporting infrastructure such as power and cooling systems providing visibility and control of complex IT infrastructures.

EcoStruxure IT is Schneider Electric's comprehensive DCIM software platform. Learn what EcoStruxure IT can do for you.
