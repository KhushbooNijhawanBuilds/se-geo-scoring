## Why choose our smart hospital room management solution?

### Reduce length of stay

Provide patients with personalized control of their room temperature, lights and blinds plus noise level monitoring, nurse call and wayfinding services to enhance their experience.

### Ensure staff satisfaction

Retain skilled staff with streamlined workflows and reduce burnout from nurse calls and alarms fatigue. People sensors help prevent patient falls and monitor unusual off-hour activity.

### Create healthy conditions

Maintain control and optimize healing environments with fresh air flow and circulation to prevent airborne contaminants following standards such as ASHRAE 170.

### Maximize efficiency

Reduce operating costs and increase energy efficiency with integrated facilities management, nurse/clinical systems, and common room management control strategies.
