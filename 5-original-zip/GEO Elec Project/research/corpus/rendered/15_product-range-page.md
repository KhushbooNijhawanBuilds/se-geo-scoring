## PacT Series

Future-proof your installation with Schneider Electric low-voltage and medium-voltage PacT Series. Built on legendary Schneider Electric innovation, the PacT Series comprises world-class circuit breakers, switches, residual current devices, and fuses, for all standard and specific applications. Experience robust performance with PacT Series within the EcoStruxure™-ready switchgear, from 16 to 6300 A in low voltage and up to 40.5 kV in medium voltage.

## ComPact

Molded-case circuit breakers

The ComPact full range of high-performance molded-case circuit breakers (MCCB) has been designed to meet your needs from thermal-magnetic to advanced MicroLogic trip units. From 16 A to 3200 A up to 690 VAC, this range features integrated earth leakage protection.

## FuPact

Fuse-switch disconnectors

FuPact is a complete range of double and single breaking fuse devices (from 32 A to 1250 A) with advanced electronic monitors for automatic short-circuit and overload fault detection.

![Fupact]

- FuPacT FuPacT Fusegear to interrupt and protect distribution lines up to 1250 A

## VigiPact

Residual current devices

VigiPact relays with associated toroid sensors measure the leakage current in electrical installation to detect levels that may be damaging. Ideal for protection or alarming, the products offer multiple setting and mounting possibilities.

- VigiPacT VigiPacT Earth-leakage protection and monitoring relays (formerly Vigirex)

## TransferPact

Source-changeover systems

The TransferPact source-changeover system with both manual (MTSE) and automatic (ATSE) transfer switching equipment has been designed for circuit breakers (Class CB) and switch disconnectors (Class PC) for up to 6300 A.
