## Achieving net-zero emissions by 2030

- BOLD Pursuit to achieve net-zero emissions by 2030

- 530 tonnes CO₂e of GHG emissions saved per year since 2021

- 18% reduction operational emissions between 2022 and 2023

## Driving sustainability at YVR

YVR is cutting emissions, boosting efficiency and introducing low‑carbon transport as part of its net zero by 2030 plan — powered by Schneider Electric EcoStruxure solutions, reducing 530t CO₂e/year since 2021 and lowering emissions by 18 % from 2022–23.
