## Peak transformer performance

Rubis Photosol is the leading independent producer of photovoltaic energy in France. Its subsidiary, Photom Services, is responsible for the engineering, management, and maintenance of their solar power infrastructure. Together, the companies operate 50 solar parks with a combined capacity of 600 MWp that is fed into the national grid.

Aiming to maintain a 99% uptime rate, Rubis Photosol has partnered with Schneider Electric through our EcoCare for Electrical Distribution Equipment service plan.

![two men in the field]

[hidden] ## Deeper insights into performance

[hidden] On solar farms, transformers are critical for safe photovoltaic operation and reliable power delivery to the grid. They face intense electrical loads with constant variations and harsh conditions that accelerate wear and increase risk failure.

[hidden] A single transformer failure can cause significant financial losses resulting from equipment replacement, downtime, and penalties from unmet Power Purchase Agreements. Rubis Photosol and Photom Services operate 250 medium-voltage transformers and needed more insightful monitoring to their health.

[hidden] ## A predictive, AI‑driven approach

[hidden] EcoCare for Electrical Distribution Equipment delivers what traditional plans can’t: AI-powered condition-based maintenance, 24/7 remote monitoring, predictive analytics, and alarm management.

[hidden] IoT sensors track hydrogen levels, temperature, and vibration with the data aggregated in the EcoStruxure™ IoT platform for a simple and predictive view of transformer health and trends. Supported by AI, our experts provide actionable recommendations to anticipate equipment issues and address them before they become critical.

[hidden] ![two men and computer screen]

[hidden] ## Tangible results in hours, not months

[hidden] The installation of IoT sensors took less than four hours, but the benefits were immediate.

[hidden] Prior analyses had flagged four at-risk transformers, but our in-depth diagnostics confirmed only one was truly at risk. This insight saved an estimated €420,000 in replacement costs and the at-risk transformer was refurbished to serve as a back-up, which saved 55 tons of CO₂ emissions and avoided a 8–12-month lead time for a new unit.

[hidden] Encouraged by their successful pilot launch, Photom Services is now expanding their EcoCare contract coverage to cover more of its sites.
