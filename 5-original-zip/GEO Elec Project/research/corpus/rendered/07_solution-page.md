## What are EcoStruxure rack systems for AI?

Optimized for next-gen architecture

EcoStruxure rack solutions support modular and scalable systems for AI. Featuring APC NetShelter products, our solutions integrate high‑density power distribution, racks, and power shelves, supporting MGX and ORV3 architectures to enhance flexibility, and enable future upgrades. This solution is part of our pod architecture for AI clusters, with NetShelter racks integrating into EcoStruxure pods for scalable, high‑density deployments.
