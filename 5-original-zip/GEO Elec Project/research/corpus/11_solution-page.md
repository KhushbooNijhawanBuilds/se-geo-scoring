# Smart hospital room management

Healthcare facilities face evolving compliance requirements, patient expectations, and resource constraints. EcoStruxure Connected Room Solutions for Healthcare provides an integrated patient experience that puts precise room control in their hands, reduces nurse calls so they can focus on caregiving, and integrates clinical and facilities systems to increase efficiency.

## A solution to address today's challenges

### 10 million

healthcare workers shortage projected by 2030¹.

### Reduce length of stay

Provide patients with personalized control of their room temperature, lights and blinds plus noise level monitoring, nurse call and wayfinding services to enhance their experience.

## Delivering value to every customer

- Ensure patient, staff and visitor safety, high-quality patient care, and data privacy.
- Secure the facility's financial health and stability while adhering to environmental and regulatory compliance issues.
- Attract, recruit, and maintain top-notch healthcare personnel.
- Enable healthcare facility owners to provide a better patient experience with digital, flexible, and scalable solutions.
- Reduce embodied carbon without redundant gateways and hardware, and modular components.
- Exceed expectations in both construction and operation phases with a comprehensive solution and simple integration with customers’ third-party systems and the IoT cloud.
- Ensure facility uptime and continuity with a modular, simple-to-install, and scalable solution for smart room control in a wide range of healthcare facility environments.
- Reduce operating costs with integrated facility management at the building management system (BMS) level to provide insights across all patient room systems.
- Configure rooms in multiple modes or scenes based on the room occupancy status.
- Integrate clinical systems at the BMS level to ensure efficient system operation and coordinate maintenance with actual room occupancy.
- Manage the entire facility and gain actionable insights with a single convenient platform and simple integration to EcoStruxure Building Operation.
- Configure spaces in multiple modes or scenes based on room status.
- Get fast time to market: install quickly and with minimal interruption or downtime to daily activities.
- Customize and easily scale the solution to meet dynamic healthcare facility space and technology needs.
