The buyer’s stage changes what matters most

StageThe buyer asks, for exampleCounts more
Awareness“Why does my site keep losing power?”A clear answer, neutral tone
Education“How does liquid cooling work?”Evidence, full explanations
Consideration“Liquid or air cooling for AI racks?”Comparisons, proof
Evaluation“Does this UPS fit a 500 kW room?”Specs, proof, compatibility
Decision“Where can I buy it, and how fast?”Availability, price, next step

Each content type has a written test for picking its stage, or you can set it yourself. Every result states the stage it used and why.

Example, on a blog: “answer up front” is worth 17.9 points at Awareness but 12.1 at Evaluation; “facts, not hype” moves the other way, 6.4 up to 14.4.
