SEO: how to run it, and what the six blogs scored

How to run it

  • Open the SEO prompt file for blogs
  • Paste it into Copilot and give the URL
  • It returns the score, every check with its quote, and the fixes
  • An extractor measures the mechanical things — title length, meta length, headings, alt text, links — so the AI never guesses them

Six Schneider blogs

Typical SEO score 72, from 49 to 75. Four of six reach Good — SEO hygiene on the blog is in better shape than its GEO readiness.

All six pass the technical check: indexable, correct canonical, HTTPS.

Common faults: every title runs 78–115 characters because of the “ - Schneider Electric Blog” suffix; meta descriptions are ~420 characters; hero images carry empty alt text.

Status: built and tested for blog articles. The other content types need their own SEO research round — product and solution pages are judged on different things, such as specs tables and product markup.

The two scores stay separate. A page that is Good for SEO but Average for GEO tells you more than an average of the two would.

Content SEO Score v1 · standard, calculator, extractor and prompt are in the project repository

Only about 38% of AI Overview citations come from pages ranking in Google's top ten, so ranking well and being quoted are genuinely different outcomes. That is why we score them separately.
