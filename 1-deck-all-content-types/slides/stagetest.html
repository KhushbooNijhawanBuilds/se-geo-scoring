How the stage is chosen — a written test for each content type

You can always set the stage yourself. If you don’t, the type’s own test runs and the result says which rule matched and why. Rules are read in order; the first match wins.

Content typeThe test, first match winsDefault
Blog articleCompares named products or gives specs for a named offer → Evaluation · compares approaches (“AC vs DC coupling”) → Consideration · explains how something works or how to do it → Education · otherwise a trend or opinion piece → AwarenessEducation
News / press releaseA launch or partnership with 3+ specs or capabilities with units → Evaluation · a launch with an offer but no specs → Consideration · a research or study release → Education · corporate, event or award → AwarenessConsideration
Solution / industry pageEvaluation only with a spec table, or 2+ named ranges each with a quantified parameter, and a proof item · otherwise ConsiderationConsideration
Service pageMainly an order, contract or price page → Decision · names plans, service levels or eligible equipment → Evaluation · otherwise exploring the approach → ConsiderationEvaluation
Product range pageA master range listing sub-ranges → Consideration · a single range whose members carry ratings with units → Evaluation · a single range with no member ratings → ConsiderationConsideration
Software pageConsideration, fixed — unless you declare otherwise. A trial page is judged at Decision, where licensing and trial terms carry 31% of the weightConsideration
Customer success storyFramed around a purchase or contract with product references → Decision · an approach or partnership showcase with no quantified result → Consideration · otherwise → EvaluationEvaluation
Calculator / TradeOff ToolThe title says it compares options (“vs”, “trade-off”, “which…”) → Consideration, even if it outputs cost · it sizes, prices or computes ROI for one option → Evaluation · it explains how a metric is calculated → EducationEvaluation
White paper pageThe title or abstract compares approaches or options → Consideration · otherwise → EducationEducation
Sustainability / ESGAlways Evaluation, unless you declare another stage — a report is read to verify figures, not to exploreEvaluation

The stage moves a score by about 10 points, so this test is what makes two people scoring the same page agree. It was the single biggest source of drift before v2.2.
