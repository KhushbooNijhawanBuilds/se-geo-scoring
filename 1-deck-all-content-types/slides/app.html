Two ways to score a page — both take minutes

1 · The scoring app

A small web app you run on your laptop. Choose the content type, paste a URL, and it returns the score, every check with its quote, the deal-breakers and the ranked fixes.

It also explains the logic for whichever content type you pick, before you score anything.

Runs locally today. A shared link can be published when the team wants one.

2 · A prompt in Copilot

No app, no licence. One ready-made prompt per content type: paste it into Microsoft Copilot or GitHub Copilot, give the page URL, and it returns the same score card.

Paste its answer back into the app to see the exact arithmetic.

26 prompts, one per content type, named in plain English.

Everything — the standard, the calculator, all 26 prompts, the app and the test results — is in one repository: github.com/KhushbooNijhawanBuilds/se-geo-scoring
