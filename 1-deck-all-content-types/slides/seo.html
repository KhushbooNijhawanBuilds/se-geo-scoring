Same method, different question

GEO asks: can an AI quote this page? SEO asks: can Google rank it? Identical machinery — yes/no checks with quotes, levels 0–5, weights to 100, deal-breakers, penalties, the same bands.

What SEO adds that GEO doesn’t check

The URL slug, title-tag length, the meta description, image alt text, Article markup — and a separate pass/fail check that the page can be indexed at all.

Kept out: keyword density, word-count targets, readability scores, tool “content scores” — the evidence doesn’t support them.

Content SEO Score v1 · blog articles · built from Google and Bing guidance, ranking studies and audit-tool rubrics

Technical and off-page items — page speed, Core Web Vitals, backlinks — are reported beside the score as “needs data”, never folded into the number, because they cannot be judged from the page itself.
