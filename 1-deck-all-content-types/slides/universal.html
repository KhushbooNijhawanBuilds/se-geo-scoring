Ten shared criteria — every content type draws on these

IDCriterionThe checklist looks for
U1Answer up frontThe answer in the first 100 words; sections open with their answer
U2What / who / when / outcomeWhat it is, who it’s for, when it applies, what result it delivers
U3Easy to scanClear headings, lists, tables, short paragraphs, self-contained sections
U4Facts, not hypeNumbers with units; no “innovative”, “industry-leading”, “seamless”
U5Proof and expertiseSourced numbers, standards by number, an expert quote, a named author
U6Follow-up questionsAnswers the question and the 3–5 likely follow-ups
U7Neutral toneAdvisor voice, honest trade-offs, no stacked calls to action
U8Consistent namesOne name per product, identifiers present, no contradictions
U9Up to dateA visible date, current facts and standard editions
U10Right depth, clear next stepFits the buyer’s stage; links to a named SE resource

Each content type uses the shared criteria that fit it, then adds a few of its own — and sets its own weights. A blog uses nine of the ten plus three blog-specific criteria.
