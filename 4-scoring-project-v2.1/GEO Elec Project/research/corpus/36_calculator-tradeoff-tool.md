### Eco-mode: Benefits and Risks of Energy-saving Modes of UPS Operation

White paper 157 discusses the benefits and risks of energy-saving modes such as eco-mode or eConversion mode in Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) systems. , Many newer UPS systems include these modes but most data centers do not utilize it.

### Guidance on What to Do with an Older UPS

White paper 214 provides guidance for deciding what to do with older uninterruptable power supplies (UPSs). The answer is not always self-evident and will depend on many factors.

### The Different Types of UPS Systems

White paper 1 describes four types of UPSs, provides practical applications of each, and discusses their advantages and disadvantages. With this knowledge, you can make an educated decision about the best UPS type for your application.
