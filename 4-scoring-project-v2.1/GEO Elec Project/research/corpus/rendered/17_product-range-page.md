## Line Series

A flexible, safe, and reliable solution for distribution systems up to 6300A. The Line Series uses a decentralized distribution architecture based on a plug-and-play concept. The design and installation time is optimized compared to a cable solution. Loads can be easily added or removed. The busbar system is easy to upgrade according to your business need. Protect personnel and property through the safety and reliability provided by Line Series busbar systems.

## Canalis

Busbar trunking system compliant with IEC

Canalis busbar trunking systems are designed for transporting and distributing electricity for power distribution systems at every level, from office buildings to heavy-duty industrial sites. The high density of connection points gives the power distribution maximum flexibility and scalability, especially in data centers.
