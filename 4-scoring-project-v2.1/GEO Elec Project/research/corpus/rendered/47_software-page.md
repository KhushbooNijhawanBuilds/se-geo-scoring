## Building management

Simplify the management and maintenance of HVAC and lighting systems to provide occupant comfort and building performance.

- Monitor and manage building systems from one convenient, mobile-enabled control center.

- Automate HVAC, lighting, and blinds based on occupancy through a building management system and sensors.

- Get predictive maintenance and prioritize activities based on key metrics, including cost, energy savings, and asset life.

## Power management

Gain real-time monitoring of your electrical power distribution to optimize system performance, reliability, and power quality.

- Maintain power availability with events analysis, real-time monitoring, and control of critical loads.

- Monitor and analyze power system and electrical distribution health for reliability, power quality, and compliance.

- Optimize energy management with advanced orchestration of on-site renewables and loads.
