## Data Centers are Essential to AI-driven Decarbonization of Power Sector

Decarbonization of energy generation and distribution is an important objective, because of sizable emissions of this sector are widespread across the entire economy. Power sector requires electrification with more renewables in energy mix. Equally important is extensive digitalization that will help managing transformation. This process will generate large amounts of data and is impossible without including data centers in infrastructure. By housing Artificial Intelligence, data centers will allow smart management of supply and demand to mitigate carbon footprint. Energy generation and distribution is going to be significantly more resilient and secure thanks to broader deployment of data centers.
