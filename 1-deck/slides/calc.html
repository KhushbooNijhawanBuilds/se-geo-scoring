Five steps turn checks into a score

Step 1

Checks

Each criterion has 3–5 checks. The judge answers pass or fail, with a quote

Step 2

Level 0–5

Calculator: share of ticks × 5, plus the rubric’s floors and caps (see slide 9)

Step 3

Weights

Weights for the page type × stage multiplier, rescaled to sum to 100

Step 4

Raw score

Points = level ÷ 5 × weight, added across criteria

Step 5

Final score

Raw minus penalties (max −10), then the lowest gate cap

THE FORMULA

raw = Σ (level ÷ 5 × weight)
final = min( lowest gate cap , raw − penalties )

STAGE EXAMPLE, BLOG

Answer-first (U1): 14 → 17.9 at Awareness, 12.1 at Evaluation. Facts (U4): 10 → 6.4 at Awareness, 14.4 at Evaluation.

The judge never produces the number, and since v2.2 it doesn’t pick the levels either. It only answers checks with evidence; code applies the levels, weights, penalties and caps, so the same answers always give the same score. Stage multipliers range from 0.5 to 1.5 per criterion family.
