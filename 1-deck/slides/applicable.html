Why not U2? It checks whether an offer says what it is, who it is for, when to use it and what result it delivers. A blog explains a topic rather than presenting an offer, so this is already covered by U1 (the answer up front) and U10 (the right audience and stage). Blogs add three checks of their own instead: ART-1 key takeaways, ART-2 title and subheads, ART-3 original insight.
