Levels are a star rating for each topic

LevelMeans
0Missing
1Barely there
2Weak
3Good enough
4Strong
5Best in class

EXAMPLE: “ANSWERS UP FRONT” ON AN SE BLOG

4 of 5 checks passed, so the calculator rates the topic 4, strong.

The topic is worth 15.8 points on this page, so it earns 4 ÷ 5 × 15.8 = 12.6 points.

The other 3.2 points are what passing the last check would win back.

Level = share of ticks × 5, rounded; five stars only when every check passes. The AI can lower a level only by naming the weak check.

Each topic has a short yes/no checklist. The calculator sets the level from the share of ticks (version 2.2), and a few written rules keep it honest: for example, if key facts sit only in an image, structure can’t score above 2. Points are the level as a fraction of five, times the topic’s weight. The points a topic misses become the fix list, ranked by value.
