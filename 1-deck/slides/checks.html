Six things readers and AI both value

1 · Answers up front

The answer is in the first lines, not in paragraph five.

2 · Facts, not hype

Numbers with units instead of “innovative” or “industry-leading”.

3 · Proof and sources

Named sources, standards, experts and real customer results.

4 · Next questions too

Answers the 3–5 follow-up questions a buyer would ask.

5 · Neutral, not salesy

Reads like an advisor and is honest about trade-offs.

6 · Clear and current

Easy to scan, visibly dated, one name per product.

Each kind of page adds a few checks of its own. A customer story must show a measured result; a product page must show its specs.
