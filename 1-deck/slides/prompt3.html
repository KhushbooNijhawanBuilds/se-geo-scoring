STEP 3 - GATES (caps on the final score; the lowest cap wins) AND PENALTIES
Gates:

   whose listed members stop at 600 A). Not material: wording or naming variation, rounding (15 A
   vs 16 A of the same value), a mislabelled card whose facts are right, or a metadata/version
   mismatch (judge that under U8 c3 / DOC-7). Widget or runtime output errors ("#VALUE!", a tool
